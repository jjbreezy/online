🚫 The Naughty List: Things Not to Get in 2026

To keep things zero-waste and avoid duplicates, here are items I already have in abundance:

  • Physical Books: I read exclusively on Kindle now. My digital queue is already full!
  • Standard Clothes: T-shirts, baseball caps, gloves, basic sweaters — closets are at max capacity. (Gift cards or specific wishlist pieces only!)
  • Wall Art & Poster Prints: Wall space in NYC is finite and fully decorated.
  • Random Tech & Cables: Chargers, generic earbuds, desk gadgets — looking to minimize e-waste.
  • Lotions & Skincare: Very sensitive skin, I source my own products.
