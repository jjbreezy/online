Guidelines & Gifting Notes

A few quick guidelines to keep gifts joyful, minimal, and clutter-free:

  • Color palette: I heavily lean toward neutrals, black, olive, slate, and charcoal.
  • Gift Cards: Always appreciated and zero-waste (Aesop, Buck Mason, Amazon, local NYC spots).
  • Please avoid: Random knick-knacks, printed wall posters, scented lotions, and physical books (I read exclusively on Kindle).
